Liora

A management system for health clinics. Each clinic runs its agenda, patient records and payments in one place, and sends reminders from its own WhatsApp number.

  • Book appointments and keep a waiting list;
  • Keep patient records with PHQ-9 and GAD-7;
  • Confirm attendance over WhatsApp;
  • Receive time requests from a public page;
  • Talk to the team in the built-in chat;
  • See reports by professional.

02Liora module

WhatsApp confirmations

Each clinic connects its own number through Meta's Embedded Signup. The day before the appointment, the patient gets a reminder with two buttons, and the answer updates the agenda.

  • Connect the clinic's number in a Meta window inside Liora;
  • Send the reminder with Confirmo presença and Preciso remarcar;
  • Mark the appointment as confirmed when the patient taps;
  • Flag reschedule requests on the agenda in amber;
  • Leave any other reply for the team to read.

Online booking

A public page where patients ask for a time. The request holds the slot until someone at the clinic accepts or declines it.

  • Share one booking link per clinic;
  • Pick a day and a free time;
  • Hold the slot while the request waits;
  • Accept it and fill in the patient form from the request;
  • Check conflicts with the same rules as the agenda.

04Liora module

Clinical scales

PHQ-9 and GAD-7 in the patient record, with the score and the range calculated on the server. Each application goes to an insert-only table.

  • Apply the scale in the patient's Scales tab;
  • See the score and the range while you answer;
  • Raise an alert when PHQ-9 item 9 is above zero;
  • Keep every application in the history with the range of that day;
  • Log every read and write in the access log.
